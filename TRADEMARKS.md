# Quox product identity and trade marks

Quox, QuoxCORE, QUOXON, Quox product names, marks, original logos and distinctive brand presentations identify software and services of Quox LTD.

## Permitted descriptive use
You may truthfully refer to Quox in product comparisons, tutorials, documentation, compatibility statements, screenshots, independent reviews, and descriptions of plugins that work with Quox, subject to applicable law. You can name and brand your own organisation, plugin, documentation and outputs without obscuring the Quox-owned platform identity.

## Restrictions for protected platform distributions
Under the [Quox Community Source Licence 1.0](LICENSE), the unmodified, official QuoxCORE product identity, attribution and product logos must be preserved. You cannot remove/rebrand those protected core marks, impersonate an official Quox release, or claim that Quox LTD endorses your independent product without permission.

A formal white-label/OEM agreement may explicitly waive designated branding restrictions for a defined use. That is a separate commercial licence, not a generally available right.

Open-source repositories may use the Quox name descriptively, and their Apache/MIT copyright licences apply to their code even when trade-mark rights remain with Quox LTD.
