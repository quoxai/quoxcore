# dev7 candidate-generation findings (2026-10-10)

Set: 70 paraphrases + 15 near-misses, authored by an independent agent from raw corpus rows (no search access). Tunable.
65 of 70 targets are visible to the owner; 5 are user-scoped memories of other users (correctly invisible, not misses).

Baseline as owner (limit 8, rerank on): hit@1 0.50, hit@3 0.61, hit@8 0.63, near-miss abstention 0.60.

Candidate rank before rerank (limit 50, rerank off), 65 visible targets:

| arm | @8 | @20 | @50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| fts | 24 | 33 | 35 |
| dense | 39 | 45 | 49 |
| hybrid | 42 | 51 | 54 |
| hybrid + LLM expansion | 36 | 46 | 55 |

Rejected levers (measured, not built):
- LLM query expansion (paraphrases + hypothetical answer): no candidate gain, final MRR 0.573 to 0.532.
- Larger fusion pool (limit 8 vs 50, pool 30 vs 150 chunks): top-8 42 vs 40, no gain.
- Codename glossary bridge (query to nearest QuoxPlan streams, FTS on their ids): recovered 2 of 14 lost targets, below the bar.
- Rerank input is already the matched chunk (not the note head).

Residual: 11 targets are found by no arm at 50. Most describe a codename-bearing note in plain words
("accountability engine" for RSP-ENG, "shell-gateway repo" for quoxbastion). Some are artefacts of the
paraphrase protocol (questions forced to avoid every distinctive term), so the real-user miss rate is lower.

Fifth lever, checked without building: a note-level "head" vector (title + first ~600 chars). For all 11
never-found targets the existing ordinal-0 chunk already starts within the first 122 chars (after the title
line), spans 400 to 1000 chars and is embedded; 5 of the 11 are single-chunk notes, so the head IS the whole
note. A head vector would duplicate an existing vector. The residual is vocabulary (plain words vs codenames),
not coverage of note openings.

## 2026-10-11: recency weighting (BRAIN2-V2 V2-7), measured, not made default

Factor 1 + w * 2^(-age/halfLife) beside the kind prior (663049e74), service-key override only.

| arm | cortex dev+dev2+dev3 key hit@3 (92) | dev7 hit@3 | dev7 MRR |
|---|---|---|---|
| off | 0.935 | 0.671 | 0.620 |
| w 0.2, half-life 30d | 0.946 | 0.657 | 0.573 |
| w 0.4, half-life 30d | 0.946 | 0.629 | 0.589 |
| w 0.3, half-life 120d | 0.957 | 0.586 | 0.495 |

Always-on recency costs more on dev7 (older notes asked about directly) than it gains on the dev sets. A
cue-gated variant (only for "latest", "current", "still"...) cannot be measured yet: 2 of 96 dev and 2 of 70
dev7 questions carry a cue. Needs a temporal tunable set (superseded decisions, "what is the current X")
before any default changes.

|---|---|---|---|---|

questions see no date at all.
