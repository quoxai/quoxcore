# Quox commercial licensing

QuoxCORE is free to self-host, including commercial internal use, under the [Quox Community Source Licence 1.0](LICENSE). Compliant users do not need a paid core licence, a hosted Quox account, or permission to build, license or sell independent plugins through the documented APIs.

Written commercial permission from Quox LTD is required to:
- offer a hosted/managed Quox platform to third-party customers;
- distribute QuoxCORE as part of an OEM product or appliance;
- remove or change Quox's product identity and logos (white label);
- redistribute or alter protected Quox-owned core code;
- sell the Quox platform itself under another name.

Paid installation or support of a customer's **own**, directly licensed official instance is not automatically a forbidden Quox-as-a-service offering; consult the [licence](LICENSE) for boundaries.

Commercial arrangements are negotiated separately and must expressly identify the permitted products, version scope, users, branding changes and distribution channel. Contact hello@quox.ai.

All four public protocols (AEE, AOCL, VOLT, WARD) and their independent open-source implementations remain under Apache License 2.0. Independently published Quox Plugin SDK components remain under MIT. Third-party software retains its own licence.

This document summarises the policy but does not replace the controlling product licence.
